About HB Power Tower

China's leading manufacturer of steel transmission towers and power infrastructure since 1994

Who We Are

HB Power Tower Co., Ltd. (Hebei Huawo Steel Structure Co., Ltd) is a leading manufacturer of steel transmission towers, monopole towers, and substation structures based in Hengshui, Hebei Province, China — the heartland of China's tower manufacturing industry.

Founded with registered capital of RMB 120.8 million, we employ 386 staff including 8 senior engineers and 36 technicians. Our annual production capacity exceeds 80,000 tons, serving clients across 30+ Chinese provinces and international markets including Southeast Asia, Middle East, Africa, and Latin America.

With over 30 years of experience, we have established ourselves as one of China's most trusted tower manufacturers, combining advanced engineering capabilities with competitive pricing and reliable delivery.

Our Journey

1994

Company Founded — Established in Hengshui, Hebei Province, beginning production of angle steel towers for domestic power utilities.

2002

ISO 9001 Certified — Achieved quality management certification and expanded to 220kV–500kV transmission tower production.

2008

First International Export — Began exporting to Southeast Asian markets. Established in-house hot-dip galvanizing facility.

2015

Capacity Expansion — Upgraded to 80,000+ tons annual capacity. Added monopole tower and tubular steel tower production lines.

2020

Triple ISO Certified — Achieved ISO 9001 + 14001 + 45001. Expanded exports to Middle East, Africa, and Latin America.

2026

Global Presence — Serving 15+ countries, 30+ domestic provinces. Continuing investment in 5G tower and smart grid infrastructure capabilities.
